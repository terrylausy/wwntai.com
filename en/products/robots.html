Mini Pi Specifications

Sleek black-and-white design with a metallic finish, standing just 520 mm tall and weighing only 7 kg — light enough to lift with one hand for development. With 12 degrees of freedom and a max joint torque of 16 Nm powered by the self-developed HTM-5047 joint module, it is an ideal platform for university robotics teaching.
