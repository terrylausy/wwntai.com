COOPERATION MODELS

Five Cooperation Models

Flexible and negotiable, from basic labs to deep research, in stages

Model 0 · Brand School-enterprise brand co-building

Joint university robot brand co-building

Co-build a university-owned robot brand — the university provides branding, space, and teams; the enterprise provides hardware, technology, and operations. IP belongs to the university.

🏫 University brand and facilities

  • University provides brand qualification and IP
  • Provides professional lab space
  • Provides faculty and student R&D teams

🦾 Enterprise technology

  • Provides OpenArm hardware
  • Provides core SDK and engineering
  • Provides production and market operations

🎯 Co-built outcomes

  • Jointly develop customized teaching robots
  • Co-create curricula with university IP
  • Campus commercialization and revenue sharing

✅ University benefits

  • Own a robot brand with independent IP
  • Talent and technology outcomes commercialized
  • Build model industry-education-research projects
Model 1 · Basic Fast deployment

Industry-education lab co-building

Focused on teaching and training to build a model lab.

📦 Hardware + curriculum

  • Provides Mini Pi / Pi+ / Mini Hi robots
  • Standard course packs
  • Lab guides, PPTs, case code libraries

👨🏫 Tiered faculty and student training

  • Faculty: operations, teaching, SDK
  • Students: motion, algorithms, secondary development

🏆 Competition support

  • Class-A competition resources
  • Past winning cases

✅ University benefits

  • Quickly build a standard embodied AI lab
  • Improve teaching and hands-on skills
  • Boost competition strength at low cost
Model 2 · Advanced Research + talent

Joint research institute

Co-build an embodied intelligence research institute focused on R&D and commercialization.

🔬 Joint R&D platform

  • University provides space and teams
  • WWNT provides hardware, engineering, SDK
  • Joint research grants

👥 Deep involvement

  • Product iteration and scenario adaptation
  • Market research and feedback
  • Top projects enter the roadmap

📚 Customized support

  • Custom teaching cases and labs
  • Ongoing support and SDK updates

✅ University benefits

  • Build a high-level research platform
  • Deep participation in real R&D
  • Accumulate differentiated outcomes
Model 3 · Deep Research-production integration

Outcome commercialization

Centered on commercialization to close the industry-education-research loop.

🚀 Joint company / incubation

  • Co-found companies or incubate projects
  • University invests technology/team/space
  • Clear IP and revenue sharing

⚙️ Engineering and deployment

  • Engineering optimization of prototypes
  • Hardware adaptation and testing
  • Mass production and sales

💼 Talent and employment

  • Order-based training
  • Internship and employment matching
  • Industry-oriented courses

✅ University benefits

  • Closed-loop commercialization
  • Build an industry-education-research benchmark
  • Broaden employment channels
