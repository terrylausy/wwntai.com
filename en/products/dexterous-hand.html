Technical Leadership

Five Core Technical Advantages

LinkerBot dexterous hands lead internationally, giving universities the most advanced end-effector platform for robot education and research.

High DOF

Up to 42 whole-hand DOF (human hand 27 / ShadowHands 26), with up to 7 DOF per finger

Multi-sensor

Vision-touch perception: fingertip + palm + wrist cameras with full electronic skin coverage

Vision-touch fusion

0.01 N 3D force precision and 1 mm resolution — multimodal perception at an internationally leading level

Edge-cloud fusion

The first web-based dexterous hand — no coding required, two ways to get started quickly

Self-developed flexible motors

Self-developed flexible joint motors with over 0.1 mm repeatability — the smallest motors in dexterous hands
