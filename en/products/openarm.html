CORE FEATURES

Key Features

01

Fully Open Source

Complete CAD drawings, firmware, control code, and simulation tools are all public. Under the CERN Open Hardware License, free secondary development and commercial licensing are supported.

02

Teleoperation & Force Feedback

Bilateral force feedback for smooth human-like control with real-time gravity compensation, ideal for contact-rich teleoperation and real-world data collection.

03

Native ROS2 Support

Native ROS2 / BrainOS middleware support for node development, topic communication, simulation debugging, and multi-robot coordination, compatible with MuJoCo and Isaac Sim.

04

Domestic Supply Chain

Deeply aligned with China's joint motor supply chain, with fully domestic IP. Manufacturing licenses are freely open to all domestic manufacturers.

05

AI Model Compatibility

Listed as an official data-collection device in Hugging Face's LeRobot framework, supporting imitation learning, RL, and VLA model deployment.

06

Flexible Configurations

Flexibly adapts to wheeled, legged, and other chassis, supporting three DIY configurations: Station, Strench, and Heavy.

CURRICULUM

Aligned Curriculum

OpenArm focuses on cross-disciplinary engineering practice, with five course categories covering core undergraduate and vocational courses.

Embedded systems / ARM embedded technology— Hardware circuits, driver development, cross-compilation, Linux setup
Microcontrollers & interface technology— GPIO, serial, CAN bus, I2C/SPI communication labs
Linux OS (embedded track)— Terminal operations, process management, service configuration
Python / C++ programming— Arm command writing, data acquisition, motion control
Sensor technology & detection— Force, attitude, and vision sensor data acquisition
Robotics / robotics fundamentals— Forward/inverse kinematics, statics, and dynamics on real hardware
Robot motion control— PID control, impedance control, trajectory planning, joint debugging
Industrial robotics— Teach programming, point control, continuous path motion, tooling
Robot drive & electrical control— Servo motors, drivers, electrical schematics, wiring
ROS / ROS2 robot operating system— Node development, topic communication, simulation, multi-robot coordination
Computer vision & image processing— Image acquisition, object detection, feature matching for vision grasping
Deep learning & robot applications— Lightweight model deployment, imitation learning, RL experiments
Human-robot interaction— Voice, vision, and force-control interaction development
Mechanisms / machine design— Multi-DOF linkage structures, covering linkages, joints, and transmission
3D modeling & mechanical innovation— Secondary design and 3D printing from OpenArm open CAD models
Integrated robotics / engineering innovation training— Cross-disciplinary projects integrating mechanics, electronics, programming, and vision
Competition coaching— National robotics competitions, vocational skills contests, and more
Open hardware & maker practice— Arm assembly, secondary development, creative projects
