Paper Shrimp Drive

A multi-skill paper U-drive workstation
Full-process assistance for undergraduate and graduate STEM and mechanical theses

Learn More View Skill Library
Product Definition

Product Definition

The Paper Shrimp Drive is a portable plug-and-play USB research workstation built on the open-source OpenClaw agent framework.

Core Positioning

With a large library of pre-installed paper Skills(AI skill plugins) for full-process STEM thesis assistance. The drive ships with a complete runtime,no local software installation required, usable across computers, labs, and libraries.

Data security

All paper data and skill configs stay on the drive,leaving no trace when unplugged. Unpublished papers, simulation data, and test data never upload to the cloud — ideal for confidential labs and offline intranets.

Hardware Base

Dual-interface drive

USB-A + Type-C, fully Windows/macOS compatible, switching freely across machines.

exFAT filesystem

Cross-device with Node.js, Python, and inference gateway — zero-config.

Dual run modes

Portable (all data on drive) / local install modes, one-click switching.

Skills Library

Built-in multi-skill paper library

Four categories tailored to your gripper and shrimp-plate mechanical projects

01

General paper Skills

Built-in · covering the full paper lifecycle

Paper outline generation

One-click full outlines for proposals, mid-terms, and theses, with mechanical/automation/aquatic templates.

Literature review

Batch-reads PDFs, extracts methods, innovations, and gaps, and generates review paragraphs with GB/T 7714, IEEE, and APA citations.

Academic polishing & humanizer

Removes AI patterns, adjusts academic phrasing, reduces AI-detection and duplication, with bilingual abstracts and SCI-style rewriting.

Formatting

Word and LaTeX thesis templates, figure/table captions, acknowledgments, appendices, and formula standardization.

Mechanical engineering Skills

Tailored to gripper and shrimp-plate device projects

Auto-writes gripper, frame, transmission, and tray design with material selection, force calculation, and drive comparison.

Dynamics / simulation

Aquatic automation

A dedicated terminology base for shrimp-plate automation, patents, and pain points to extract innovations.

Experimental data

Imports breakage rate, success rate, and capacity data, auto-cleaning and generating experimental conclusions.

Experiment logs

Device diagrams and LaTeX formula formatting.

Innovation mining

Analyzes existing-scheme flaws, extracts innovations, and generates patent frameworks.

Skill expansion

Data security

Supports drive encryption; nothing syncs to cloud, complying with research confidentiality.
