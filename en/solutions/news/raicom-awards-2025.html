2025: Guangzhou Huashang students win national awards using the WWNT training kit

October 2025 (awards)
9 national awards
Guangzhou Huashang College School of AI
Official award certificates

Institution: Guangzhou Huashang College | Event: AI Vision Application | Award: Second Prize

In the 2025 RAICOM robot developer competition, Guangzhou Huashang College students, powered by theWWNT multi-functional training platform, stood out among dozens of top universities to win9 national awards, including2 first prizes, 3 second prizes, and 4 third prizes, across AI vision and humanoid robot tracks.

RAICOM: a national Class-A competition

RAICOM is co-hosted by theMIIT Talent Exchange Center, a national Class-A event listed in the national college competition analysis report asClass-A, themed"machines caring for people".

Founded in 2015 as RoboCom and renamed in 2023, it has held10 editions, becoming a top platform linking talent and industry and acore benchmark。

Each year it gathers elite teams fromTsinghua, Peking, Zhejiang, SJTU, HIT, and NUDTand dozens of top universities. Winning alongside them is high recognition of Huashang's teaching and student innovation.
