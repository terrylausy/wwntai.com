Embodied AI Robots

HighTorque strategic partner, offering three high-performance robot lines

Mini Pi

Bipedal Robot

520mm 7kg 12 DOF 16Nm

A lightweight, portable bipedal platform you can hold in one hand, with self-developed joint modules and dual-encoder brushless motors — ideal for research entry and teaching.

Mini Pi+

Modular Open Platform

650mm 10.15kg 22 DOF Orin NX

An official RoboCup platform with open SDK, RealSense + Orin NX, supporting jumping and whole-body control for competition and research.

Mini Hi

High-performance Humanoid

830mm 20kg 23 DOF 20 kg payload

A cost-effective humanoid with bionic joints, supporting walking, jumping, and obstacle crossing, powered by Orin NX for research.
