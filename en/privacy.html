I. How We Collect and Use Your Personal Information

Personal information is any information recorded electronically or otherwise that can identify a specific natural person alone or in combination with other information. We collect and use your personal information for the purposes described in this Policy.

(I) Website Forms and Service Information

Account registration and website forms

When registering an account or filling in website forms, we collect your name, phone, email and verification code, company/university name, industry, and region. This information is used for identity verification, account management, business contact, issue response, consultation push, and follow-up.

Event and customer-service interactions

Surveys and communication records from online events, questionnaires, or support channels may contain your name/user ID, phone, email, company/university, and employee/student ID — used only for event coordination, issue handling, and follow-up.

Voluntary information and user management

You may voluntarily provide a nickname, gender, birthday, and location. We may build user profiles to offer more personalized products and services. If we have reasonable grounds that your profile or content violates laws or platform rules, we may modify content and suspend or terminate your account and services.

(II) Operational Log Information

When you visit this site, we may automatically collect web log information via cookies and similar technologies for statistics, experience optimization, stability, and network security. Logs include browsing history, network status, visit date/time, device identifiers, and behavior logs (visits and downloads).

(III) Continuous Product Development and Optimization

We use collected information to support operations, such as evaluating, maintaining, and improving product performance. For developing new products and services, we may statistically analyze information, but the resulting analysis contains no personally identifying information.

II. How We Use Cookies and Similar Technologies

(I) Cookies

To ensure the site works properly, we store small data files called cookies on your computer or mobile device. Cookies usually contain identifiers, site names, and other characters. Cookies help verify your identity and secure data and services. We do not use cookies for any purpose beyond those described in this Policy.

(II) Web Beacons and Pixel Tags

In addition to cookies, we may use web beacons, pixel tags, and similar technologies. For example, emails we send may contain links back to this site.

III. How We Share, Transfer, and Publicly Disclose Your Information

(I) Sharing

We do not share your personal information with any company, organization, or individual outside Wanwu Nita, except:

  • with your explicit prior consent or authorization;
  • at your active request;
  • as stipulated in agreements signed with you (including online e-agreements and platform rules) or other legal documents;
  • as required by laws and regulations or mandatory administrative/judicial requirements;

We may share your information with Wanwu Nita affiliates, but only necessary information and within the purposes stated here. Affiliates must seek your consent again to change processing purposes;

We may share your information with partners that comply with our privacy requirements, but only necessary information. Partners must seek your consent again to change purposes.

We sign strict confidentiality agreements with companies, organizations, and individuals with whom we share information, requiring them to follow our instructions, this Policy, and necessary security measures.

Please note and confirm:When you voluntarily disclose or share personal or other information with third parties, that information may be read, collected, and used by any third party. Please carefully consider posting content containing personal information. Wanwu Nita bears no legal liability for consequences arising from your disclosure or sharing.

(II) Transfer

Transfer means handing control of personal information to another company, organization, or individual. We do not transfer your information except in the following cases:

  • with your explicit prior consent or authorization;
  • at your active request;
  • as stipulated in agreements signed with you (including online e-agreements and platform rules) or other legal documents;
  • as required by laws and regulations or mandatory administrative/judicial requirements;
  • In mergers, acquisitions, bankruptcy, asset transfers, or similar transactions involving personal information, we require the new entity to remain bound by this Policy; otherwise, it must seek your consent again.

(III) Public Disclosure

Public disclosure means publishing information to the public. We only publicly disclose your information in the following cases:

  • with your explicit prior consent or authorization;
  • as required by laws and regulations or mandatory administrative/judicial requirements.

(IV) Exceptions to Consent

Under applicable laws, we may share, transfer, or publicly disclose your personal information without prior consent in the following cases:

  • related to national security or defense;
  • related to public safety, public health, or major public interests;
  • related to criminal investigation, prosecution, trial, and enforcement of judgments;
  • to protect your or others' life, property, or other major lawful interests when consent is difficult to obtain;
  • personal information you have voluntarily made public;
  • personal information collected from legally public sources such as news reports and government disclosures;
  • necessary to sign a contract at your request;
  • necessary to maintain secure and stable service operation;
  • related to our fulfillment of legal obligations;
  • other cases prescribed by law.

Please note that under current laws and regulations, sharing or transferring anonymized personal information that cannot be restored or re-identified does not require separate notice or consent.

IV. How We Protect Your Information

We use security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, use, modification, damage, or loss. We take all reasonable measures — trusted protection mechanisms against attacks, access controls limiting access to authorized personnel, security and privacy training, and strict security and compliance systems with dedicated teams.

We take reasonable measures to avoid collecting irrelevant information, retaining your information only as long as needed for the purposes described, unless extension is required or permitted by law.

If our products, sites, or services cease operation, we will stop collecting information, notify you via push or announcements, and delete or anonymize your information within a reasonable period, unless otherwise required by law.

The Internet is not absolutely secure, and email and instant messaging are not encrypted. We strongly advise against sending personal information through such channels. Use strong passwords to help keep your account secure. We will do our best to ensure the security of any information you send us.

We remind you of possible Internet risks: breaches of our physical, technical, or administrative safeguards may lead to unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of information.

In the unfortunate event of a security incident, we will inform you promptly of the basic facts, possible impact, measures taken or to be taken, risk-reduction suggestions, and remedies. We will notify you by email, letter, phone, or push notification, or publish an announcement when individual notification is impractical, and proactively report to regulators as required.

V. Your Rights

In accordance with Chinese laws, regulations, and standards, as well as common practices in other countries and regions, we protect your rights to:

(I) Access Your Personal Information

You have the right to access your personal information under laws and regulations. You may access it yourself — for account information, access or edit your profile, change your password, add security info, or close your account.

(II) Correct Your Personal Information

If you find errors in the personal information we process, you have the right to request correction. You may correct it via our web forms, or email Support@wwntai.com for help.

(III) Delete Your Personal Information

You may request deletion of your personal information in the following cases:

  • if our processing violates laws and regulations;
  • if we collected or used your information without your consent;
  • if our processing breaches our agreement with you;
  • if you no longer use our products or services, or have canceled your account;
  • if we no longer provide products or services to you.

If we honor your deletion request, we will also notify entities that received your information to delete it promptly, unless otherwise required by law or they have your independent authorization.

After deletion, due to legal and technical limits, we may not be able to immediately delete information from backups. We will store and isolate it securely until the backup can be purged or anonymized.

(IV) Change the Scope of Your Consent

Some functions require basic personal information (see Part I). For additional information collection and use, you may grant or withdraw consent at any time by emailing Support@wwntai.com.

After withdrawing consent, we will stop processing the corresponding information, but prior processing based on your earlier authorization is not affected.

(V) Obtain a Copy of Your Personal Information

You have the right to obtain a copy of your personal information by emailing Support@wwntai.com.

(VI) Respond to Your Requests

For security, you may need to provide a written request or otherwise prove your identity. We may verify your identity before processing your request and will respond within 15 days.

If dissatisfied, you may file a complaint by emailing Support@wwntai.com.

Reasonable requests are free of charge; requests beyond reasonable limits may incur costs. We may refuse requests that are unreasonably repetitive, require excessive technical means, risk others' lawful rights, or are impractical.

In the following cases, as required by laws and regulations, we cannot respond to your requests:

  • directly related to national security or defense;
  • directly related to public safety, public health, or major public interests;
  • directly related to criminal investigation, prosecution, trial, and enforcement of judgments;
  • where there is sufficient evidence of your bad faith or abuse of rights;
  • where responding would seriously harm your or others' lawful rights;
  • involving trade secrets;
  • related to our fulfillment of legal obligations.

VI. How We Handle Minors' Information

Our website and services are also intended for students. Minors may not create accounts or provide personal information without verifiable parental or guardian consent, unless permitted by applicable law.

For minors' information collected with parental consent, we only use or disclose it where permitted by law, with explicit parental consent, or as necessary to protect minors.

Although local laws define minors differently, we treat anyone under 18 as a minor.

If we discover we collected minors' information without verifiable parental consent, we will delete it as soon as possible.

If you are a minor's parent or guardian with questions about data processing, please contact us promptly.

VII. Cross-border Transfer and Storage Period

In principle, personal information collected and generated within China is stored within China, except where cross-border transfer is permitted by applicable law.

If business needs require transferring your information outside your jurisdiction — whether to affiliates or third-party providers — we will comply with applicable law. Such jurisdictions may have different or no data protection laws. Before any cross-border transfer, we will strictly meet legal and regulatory prerequisites and fulfill obligations, including informing you of the recipient's name and contact, purposes and methods, categories of information, how you can exercise rights, and obtaining your separate explicit consent. We ensure your information receives protection equivalent to that in China, for example by seeking your consent or de-identifying data before transfer.

Generally, we retain your information only for the minimum period necessary, unless you agree to extend or the law permits. We may adjust retention periods to comply with legal requirements in the following cases:

  • to comply with applicable laws and regulations;
  • to comply with court judgments, rulings, or other legal proceedings;
  • to comply with requirements of government agencies or authorized organizations;
  • where we have reason to believe compliance is necessary;
  • to perform service agreements or this Policy, safeguard public interests, and protect the personal or property safety and lawful rights of our customers, us, our affiliates, other users, or employees.

If our products or services cease operation, we will notify you via push notifications or announcements and delete or anonymize your information within a reasonable period.

To protect your rights, we update this Policy in line with laws and business changes. If changes materially reduce your rights, we will publish them on this page and notify via announcements; if purposes, methods, or information categories change, we will seek your consent again. Major changes will receive more prominent notice.

Major changes include but are not limited to:

  • major changes to our service model, such as purposes, types, or uses of personal information;
  • major changes in ownership or organization, such as business restructuring, bankruptcy, or M&A;
  • changes to the main recipients of shared, transferred, or disclosed information;
  • major changes to your rights and how to exercise them;
  • changes to the department, contacts, or complaint channels responsible for information security;
  • when security impact assessments indicate high risk.

Continuing to use our website after an update takes effect constitutes your full reading, understanding, and acceptance of the updated Policy.
