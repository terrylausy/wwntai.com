New industry-education results! WWNT Robotics and Shenzhen University launch an AI smart course project

Nov 28, 2025
Shenzhen · SU School of Physics and Optoelectronics
WWNT Robotics & Shenzhen Suiguangming × Shenzhen University
×

SU faculty and WWNT/Suiguangming R&D teams in project discussions

Correspondents:SU Physics & Optoelectronics Office, WWNT and Suiguangming Education Division

Introduction:In November 2025, Shenzhen University's School of Physics and Optoelectronics completed its "AI-empowered smart course" project, won by WWNT Robotics (Guangzhou). Centered on the self-developed teaching agent, both sides built a full digital flagship course system for optoelectronics and AI graduates using AIGC and multimodal technology.

I. Deep school-enterprise alignment on the AI course plan

Early on, SU teaching leads and graduate faculty visited the WWNT/Suiguangming Shenzhen R&D center for technical negotiations and solution discussions.

The R&D team demoed Yanshu AI's full features, tailoring a plan for optics and computer vision courses; the university set three core needs (flagship courses, digital research courses, refined learning analytics) and four modules (slide beautification, video editing, knowledge graphs, in-class Q&A).

Photo 1: SU faculty and WWNT/Suiguangming R&D and curriculum leads in discussion, with a live Yanshu AI demo and requirements review

II. Yanshu AI deployment: transforming graduate courses

The project delivered the Yanshu AI course-production module covering the full digital chain:

Smart material processing

The agent auto-beautifies PPTs, edits videos, and generates synced subtitles, greatly reducing repetitive work; it also builds visual knowledge graphs from keywords.

Optoelectronics-specific RAG knowledge base

A local knowledge base for optics, sensor fusion, and machine vision integrates years of courseware, lab guides, and literature, enabling real-time Q&A.

Learning analytics visualization

Auto-captures interaction, exercise, and video-watch data to generate class/individual analytics, helping teachers pinpoint weaknesses and adjust pacing.

University feedback:Faculty said Yanshu AI automates repetitive teaching work, freeing time for lab guidance and research innovation.

Photo 2: Yanshu AI backend showing knowledge graphs, learning analytics, and subtitle generation

III. Value: a model for blended STEM teaching

The project brings multiple improvements to SU graduate teaching:

  • Efficiency:AI replaces 80%+ repetitive course production and grading, reducing faculty burden;
  • Resource accumulation:A unified digital resource library for reusable, iterable content;
  • Quality:Online preview + offline labs form a closed loop suited to graduate research;
  • Demonstration:A replicable AI flagship course template to be extended to undergraduate courses.

Project leads said:Yanshu AI is the software core of the "hardware + curriculum + digital platform" solution, seamlessly integrating the AI training kit, humanoid robots, and dexterous hands for unified management.

Photo 3: SU teaching team and project group at delivery, with Yanshu AI license and teaching resources handed over

IV. Long-term collaboration plan

Both sides reached a long-term cooperation intent with three follow-ups:

  • Extend digital transformation to undergraduate AI and optoelectronics courses;
  • Co-build an AI optoelectronics lab with the training kit for vision and optical detection;
  • Jointly apply for new-engineering and digital teaching reform grants.

Conclusion

This project is a model of industry-education integration in graduate digital teaching. WWNT and Suiguangming will keep iterating Yanshu AI to provide integrated digital course + smart training solutions nationwide.
