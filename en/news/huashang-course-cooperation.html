Deep Industry-Education Integration | WWNT Robotics and Guangzhou Huashang College co-build AI kits and embodied AI curriculum

Mar 2, 2026
Guangzhou · Zengcheng
WWNT Robotics × Guangzhou Huashang College

As AI and embodied intelligence evolve rapidly, university curricula face two core challenges: no standardized cutting-edge hardware platform (equipment disconnected from industry) and no systematic course resources matched to hardware ("equipment without courses, courses without labs"). Guangzhou Huashang College's School of AI partnered with WWNT Robotics on a "hardware + curriculum + labs" path to close the theory-to-practice loop.

Background: two teaching pain points

Pain 1: hardware disconnect

No standardized cutting-edge hardware platform; equipment disconnected from industry

Pain 2: missing courses

No systematic course resources matched to hardware

The School of AI reached a strategic partnership with WWNT Robotics around"hardware + curriculum + labs" three-in-onepath, closing the theory-to-practice talent loop.

Four-in-one Deep Cooperation Framework

This partnership goes beyond the traditional "university buys, enterprise delivers" model, establishing"hardware + curriculum + faculty training + lab resources" four-in-onedeep cooperation framework:

AI training kit track

6 core courses and 36 lab projects in the regular curriculum, linking theory and practice

Embodied robot track

Three robots matched to grades, forming a progressive training path

Faculty training

Full faculty training and support for proficient teaching

Lab resources

Full delivery of lab guides, PPTs, and case code libraries

Future Outlook

  • Continuously improve curricula and update lab content with industry trends
  • Expand coverage to more related majors
  • Promote joint innovation and competition incubation
  • Guided by "hardware empowers education, courses connect industry," build a Greater Bay Area AI and embodied intelligence talent benchmark and advance new-engineering reform

"With hardware as the foundation and courses as the enabler, now is the time for industry-education integration.
