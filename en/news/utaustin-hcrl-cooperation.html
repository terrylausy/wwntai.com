WWNT Robotics and UT Austin HCRL reach humanoid robot research cooperation

Sep 13, 2026
Guangzhou · Austin
WWNT Robotics × UT-Austin HCRL
×

Sep 13, 2026, Guangzhou — WWNT Robotics (wwntAI) today announced a research partnership with the UT Austin Human-Centered Robotics Laboratory (HCRL), led by Prof. Luis Sentis. Under the agreement, WWNT will provide HCRL with Mini Pi Plus and Mini Pi Plus Pro humanoid platforms for academic research with hardware-level technical support.

Key highlights

  • Providing HCRL with Mini Pi Plus and Plus Pro platforms marks WWNT's formal entry into the US academic research market.

  • Milestones: docs delivered, hardware shipped, and Prof. Luis Sentis will meet WWNT founder Henry IP at IROS 2026 to discuss further cooperation.

Milestones

Technical docs delivered

In late August, WWNT delivered a full hardware support documentation package to Prof. Sentis' team and Austin partners

Hardware shipped

Mini Pi Plus / Plus Pro robots are en route, expected in Austin in late September 2026

IROS 2026 meeting confirmed

Prof. Sentis confirmed a visit to the WWNT booth at IEEE/RSJ IROS 2026 in Pittsburgh, Sep 27 – Oct 1

On-site demo proposed

WWNT proposed a return visit to HCRL on Oct 2 for free on-site demos and support

A key milestone in WWNT's North America strategy

This partnership is a key step in WWNT's North America expansion. As HighTorque's exclusive North America partner for Mini Pi humanoids and joint modules, WWNT is delivering research-grade hardware directly to top US robotics labs with full after-sales support.

This cooperation upholds the"academics first"principle: WWNT provides hardware and support for research only, letting teams focus on frontier research.

"Placing our Mini Pi Plus humanoid platform in one of the world's top human-centered robotics labs is a key step in our North America strategy. We are honored to support Prof. Sentis and his team and look forward to meeting them at IROS 2026 in Pittsburgh."

关于合作双方

WWNT Robotics机器人（WWNT Robotics）

专注科研级人形机器人硬件、具身智能教育与高校 AI/机器人专业建设赋能。公司是高擎动力（HighTorque）Mini Pi 系列人形机器人及关节模组的北美独家合作伙伴，也是完全开源的 ROS 2 人形机械臂平台 OpenArm 的开发商。

UT-Austin Human Centered Robotics Lab（HCRL）

由 Luis Sentis 教授领导，专注于全身控制、任务与运动规划、人因工程，以及人形机器人、移动操作机器人与外骨骼的实验研究。Sentis 教授因开创人形机器人全身控制领域而享誉国际，曾参与设计并测试 NASA 的 Valkyrie 人形机器人。
