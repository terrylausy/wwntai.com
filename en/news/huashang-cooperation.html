A new chapter in industry-education! WWNT Robotics and Guangzhou Huashang College reach a full AI robotics strategic partnership

Aug 16, 2025
Guangzhou
WWNT Robotics × Guangzhou Huashang College

Recently, WWNT Robotics (Guangzhou) and Guangzhou Huashang College reached a deep strategic partnership, finalizing an integrated plan for the full AI teaching hardware line and curricula covering vocational, undergraduate, and graduate levels.

Negotiations: high-level exchanges

A delegation led by VP Lv Junmin visited WWNT headquarters; founder Ye Zhiqing and the core team hosted them. Both sides discussed the self-developedHuaxiao AIAI voice assistant,AI training kit、OpenArm teaching arm、embodied teaching robot、Yanshu AIfive core product lines, detailing curriculum, labs, faculty, competitions, and joint research.

Hands-on experience, high praise

The college team tried the full equipment line, examininghardware architecture, tiered courses, simulation platforms, and competition support, and highly praised the integrated solution. The college noted the system perfectly matches its AI and robotics talent needs.

College voice: VP Lv Junmin

"As AI and embodied robotics industries boom, the market urgently needs talent with both theory and hands-on skills. Deepening industry-education integration is central to our new-engineering programs. WWNT Robotics has complete self-developed AI hardware, standardized tiered courses, and mature deployment services — from basic AI training equipment to advanced humanoid dexterous robots — perfectly matching our talent goals."

VP Lv Junmin noted the college's existing equipment suffersinsufficient cutting-edge hardware, fragmented courses, and weak competition supportThis cooperation fills practical teaching gaps, bringing frontline technology and real projects into classrooms. He expects both sides to use the five products toco-build standardized labs, co-develop courses, and cultivate dual-qualified faculty，opening"classroom teaching — project training — competitions — employment"a complete education chain, letting students access mainstream industry platforms on campus and be job-ready on graduation.

Meanwhile, the college will leveragethe robotics institute's research strength，jointly apply for teaching and research grants, promoting two-way commercialization of outcomes and innovation to build a Greater Bay Area school-enterprise benchmark.

Five teaching products building a full-cycle training base

This cooperation covers the full AI robotics teaching product matrix:

「Huaxiao AI」AI voice assistant

adapted to classroom acoustics, with a customizable knowledge base and NLP APIs for natural-language and LLM labs

AI training kit

integrating sensing, vision, and embedded modules with no complex wiring for zero-basis AI practice

OpenArm teaching arm

a lightweight safe body with kinematics and vision-grasping cases, compatible with mainstream ROS

embodied teaching robot

Built on the leading dexterous hand platform, supporting multimodal tactile perception, humanoid interaction, and imitation learning

Yanshu AI teaching platform

enabling device cluster management, task dispatch, data collection, and report generation for integrated online-offline teaching

Full-cycle faculty empowerment lowering the teaching barrier

The talks also finalized ongoing faculty development. After equipment delivery, WWNT will send teaching teams for systematic training coveringdevice deployment, programming, lab design, and troubleshootingwith complete PPTs, lab guides, and open-source code libraries to quickly build faculty teams and reduce R&D pressure.

"This partnership with Huashang is a key move in our Greater Bay Area new-engineering strategy. We will leverage our full-stack capability to deploy mature, standardized training systems on campus — hardware as the carrier, curriculum as the core, service as the support — realizing an integrated closed loop and cultivating well-rounded AI robotics talent."

— WWNT Robotics founder Ye Zhiqing

long-term coordination to build a regional industry-education benchmark

Under the agreed plan, both sides will advance in stagesbatch equipment delivery, faculty training, standardized courses, joint AI labs, competition support, joint research, and employment matchingand related work.

Leveraging WWNT's product R&D, curriculum, and competition support with Huashang's faculty, students, and robotics institute, both sides will form a complementary, long-term mechanism to co-builda Greater Bay Area AI industry-education model，while delivering ready-to-deploy one-stop AI robotics solutions to institutions nationwide.
