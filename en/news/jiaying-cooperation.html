WWNT Robotics and Jiaying University School of Computer Science explore new AI and embodied intelligence talent models

June 18, 2026
Meizhou · Jiaying University
WWNT Robotics × Jiaying University School of Computer Science
×

On June 18, 2026, WWNT Robotics' co-founder and technical team visited Jiaying University's School of Computer Science in Meizhou for in-depth talks on AI and embodied intelligence talent and teaching, reaching multiple consensuses.

A century-old institution seeks transformation

Jiaying University, in the "Hakka capital" Meizhou, is transitioning its School of Computer Science from"traditional software" to "intelligence +"— a critical stage. Leaders outlined current programs, faculty, and labs, and the challenges ahead:

Core pain points

  • lab hardware platformsscarce, unable to support cutting-edge practice
  • companion teaching resourceslacking, without systematic courses and lab guides
  • lacking a complete path fromhardware cognition to real-robot deploymentof teaching

WWNT Robotics co-founder statement

The School of Computer Science has irreplaceable strengths in eastern Guangdong IT talent. The company will use theAI embedded training kitandembodied humanoid robotto build a four-in-one "hardware + curriculum + faculty training + student practice" solution.

Hands-on demos earn praise

After the talks, the WWNT team demoed two core products for leaders and faculty to experience first-hand.

AI embedded training kit: learning by doing

Built on Raspberry Pi 5 with camera capture and real-time YOLO inference, the whole flow takes justfive minutes。

Magnetic modular design

six modules flexibly combined

cross-grade adaptation

full coverage from freshman to senior

6 courses / 36 labs / 100+ projects

complete skill progression

Faculty admitted this"boot-and-run"teaching model brings aqualitative leap。

Embodied humanoid: from code to motion

With self-developed precision joints and controllers, multi-mode control, and control frequency over1000Hz, position precision at19-bit encoding。

Gait demos

ROS2 node communication

LiDAR mapping

From low-level motor SDK to ROS2, the toolchain builds a"low-barrier, high-ceiling"learning path.

Faculty remarked that students canenter real development earlier, boosting thesis quality and employabilitysignificantly。

Four consensuses reached

After two hours of talks, both sides reached four cooperation intents:

Consensus 1

Smart lab co-building

Build an AI innovation lab with core products, creating a"teaching-experiment-innovation-competition"integrated practice space.

Consensus 2

Undergraduate curriculum co-building

Co-develop undergraduate AI and embodied intelligence courses coveringembedded AI and robot operating systems.

Consensus 3

Dual-qualified faculty

Provide faculty withproduct and teaching-method training, supporting enterprise research visits.

Consensus 4

Student innovation incubation

Encourage student innovation on the platform and recommend top projects forhigh-level competitions。

Timeline

Both sides willsign the framework agreement soon, aiming tobefore fall 2026complete the first equipment deployment for course launch.

Joining hands to revitalize Jiaying in the intelligent era

"What we brought are real, teachable tools. Jiaying University's pragmatic attitude gives us great confidence in this partnership."

"WWNT Robotics'hardware + curriculum + service'education philosophy that truly fits frontline teaching needs."

—— Jiaying UniversitySchool of Computer Scienceleadership

Going forward, both sides will use this cooperation to explore practical undergraduate AI education integration, boosting AI talent in eastern Guangdong so thata century-old Jiaying revitalized in the intelligent era。
