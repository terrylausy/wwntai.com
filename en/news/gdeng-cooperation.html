AI Empowering Vocational Education | WWNT Robotics and Guangdong Engineering Polytechnic reach multiple cooperation intents

June 5, 2026
Guangdong Engineering Polytechnic (Guangzhou)
WWNT Robotics × Guangdong Engineering Polytechnic
Guangdong Engineering
Polytechnic ×

On June 5, 2026, a WWNT Robotics (Guangzhou) delegation visited Guangdong Engineering Polytechnic for in-depth cooperation talks on AI and embodied intelligence curriculum, lab platforms, and faculty development. They demoed the AI embedded training kit and humanoid robot, reaching multiple cooperation intents.

I. In-depth Discussion on a New AI Vocational Paradigm

On the afternoon of June 5, WWNT Robotics' co-founder and technical team visited the polytechnic for a focused symposium centered onthe deployment of AI and humanoid robots in vocational engineering programs, exchanging views on lab upgrades, course development, faculty training, and student competitions.

University pain points

  • AI / robotics-relatedoutdated training equipment
  • companionscarce training courses
  • cutting-edgeinsufficient hands-on projects
  • urgently needingindustry-grade standardized hardwareand complete teaching resources

The WWNT team detailed its full hardware line and mature curriculum and lab cases from multiple universities, reaching a consensus:

University strengths

Complete engineering programs and solid student base

Enterprise strengths

Self-developed hardware, turnkey courses, and full competition support

Photo: WWNT team and IT lab faculty discuss the AI training platform plan

II. Live Demo of Two Core Platforms

After the symposium, WWNT engineers demoed two flagship teaching devices, showing classroom, course-design, and competition capabilities.

(I) AI Embedded Training Kit

Powered by Raspberry Pi 5 with six magnetic vision/voice/sensing modules and a 15.6-inch touchscreen — ready out of the box.

Real-time object detection

Image classification

Edge model deployment

A live demo of real-time detection, classification, and lightweight edge deployment — simple and suited to vocational AI, embedded, and mechatronics courses.

IT lab faculty tried it hands-on, praising the device'smodular design, companioncomplete lab guides, which integrate into existing classes and greatly reduce prep and debugging.

Photo: engineer demonstrates the vision recognition lab on the AI training kit

(II) Embodied Humanoid Robot

Demonstrating the open-source Mini Pi humanoid with self-developed servo joints, 1000Hz control, four drive modes, and full ROS2 SDK and URDF models.

Autonomous gait

Multi-joint coordinated grasping

Human-robot interaction motions

Live demos of gait, coordinated grasping, and interaction, with explanations of kinematics, RL, and imitation learning.

Faculty asked aboutrobot teaching adaptation, project development, and competition modifications, answered in detail.

Photo: faculty observe the humanoid motion demo up close

III. Four Cooperation Areas Reached

The talks, anchored on hardware and turnkey courses, produced four clear directions:

Area 1

Training lab co-building

Build a new AI training lab with the kit and humanoid, upgrading existing conditions to create a"teaching + course design + innovation competition"integrated training base.

Area 2

Tiered course co-development

Based on WWNT Robotics'6 standard core courses, 100+ tiered lab cases, tailored to the college's IT and mechatronics programs.

Area 3

Systematic faculty development

Targeted training for all IT lab faculty coveringdevice operation, programming, lab design, and competition coaching, building a dual-qualified AI-robotics faculty team.

Area 4

Student competition support

Using the full hardware as a competition base, co-host campus AI contests and providealgorithm templates and technical support, coaching students for provincial and national competitions.

IV. Outlook — A Model AI Training Base for Guangdong

"Upgrading AI and embodied intelligence training platforms is a key part of our digital teaching reform. WWNT Robotics' integrated hardware-software solution perfectly matches our lab upgrade needs, and we look forward to filling our practical-training gaps and boosting student engineering and innovation skills."

"will'hardware as the base, curriculum as the core, service as the guarantee'fully supporting the IT lab upgrade with continuous resources, long-term technical maintenance, and industry internship channels to build a Guangdong model of AI industry-education integration."

— WWNT Robotics Co-founder

Deep industry-education・intelligence for the future

jointly upgrading the IT training base and opening an AI practical path for engineering students.
