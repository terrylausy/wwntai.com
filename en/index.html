Founded in 2021, WWNT Robotics focuses on the embodied intelligence + AI education track, serving vocational and applied undergraduate institutions nationwide. Built on five self-developed hardware platforms — Embodied AI Robots, MicroDuck open-source bipedal robot duck, OpenArm open-source robotic arm, Dexterous Hand, and Multi-functional Training Platform — paired with the Yanshu AI course builder, we connect technology deployment, industrial practice, and talent development in both directions.
