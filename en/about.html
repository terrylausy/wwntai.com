Core Business

Three Business Directions

From hardware platforms to curricula, from research support to competition services — one-stop solutions.

Embodied Robot Platform

Built on HighTorque Mini Pi / Pi+ / Hi robots, with open APIs, SDK docs, and sample code to help universities quickly build robot teaching and research environments.

  • Full line of bipedal / quadruped / humanoid robots
  • Complete SDK + API for secondary development

About Us

Mimic Nature,Forge Academic Power

wwntAI.com is an AI + robotics education platform for universities, headquartered in Guangzhou and founded in 2021. With compact embodied robots as the carrier, we integrate self-developed curricula and industrial resources for full-chain solutions from hardware to employment.

Our mission is to make AI education accessible to every university and let every student operate cutting-edge embodied robots.
